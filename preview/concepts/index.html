Every world has the same two kinds of puzzle. These world pictures were drawn by ChatGPT.

Plushie Island

Plush penguins, monkeys and puppies come alive in a toy-block village.

Feeding (sharing)
Share the snacks fairly: 12 fish between 3 plush penguins is 4 fish each.
Crates (grouping)
Pack the toy crates: 12 buttons in crates of 3 fills 4 crates.

Ice & Jungle Islands

Penguins on the ice island, monkeys in the jungle, and puppies running the ferry between them.

Feeding (sharing)
Share the food fairly: 15 bananas between 5 monkeys is 3 bananas each.
Crates (grouping)
The puppies' ferry carries crates: 20 fish in crates of 4 fills 5 crates.

Sky Islands

Floating block islands where blocky penguins, monkeys and puppies build upwards.

Feeding (sharing)
Share the berries fairly: 18 berries between 6 puppies is 3 berries each.
Crates (grouping)
Stack crates to build upwards: 24 blocks in crates of 6 makes 4 crates.
